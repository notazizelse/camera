What this does and doesn't do

  • No footage leaves the kitchen. A computer beside the camera counts people and sends one number.
  • No faces, no names, no recording. Frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately.
  • No tracking of you. This page stores nothing about who is reading it.
  • The queue count is public. The picture is not. Live view needs the access code the school gives out.
  • Never any sound. Audio is dropped at the camera and never sent.
  • Nothing is recorded. The server holds a few seconds in memory and overwrites it continuously — there is no archive to request, share, or leak.
